THE TRINITY — theme music folder
=====================================

The site currently streams calm lo-fi beds (Kevin MacLeod, incompetech.com,
CC-BY 4.0 — credited in the site footer) with no player UI: headless ambience
that starts muted and fades in on first interaction.

Drop your own tracks here as messi.mp3 / ronaldo.mp3 / neymar.mp3 and point
the `music.url` fields in data/players.ts at them to swap. Keep replacements
mellow — the mix is a bed, not a club.
